Dialog layout, spacing and inputs

Measured from the running build, then checked against Material 3, Carbon, Primer, Fluent 2, Apple HIG and Atlassian. Two of the problems turned out to be defects with one right answer, so they are fixed in every option below rather than offered as a choice. What is left is three real decisions.

Found in the build, not in a style guide

The typed field has no styling at all

The console styles text fields with input[type='text']. Our field has no type attribute, so it matches nothing: it renders as the browser's own box — 27px tall, 2px inset bevel, square corners, 14px text, where every other field in the console is 40px with a 1px hairline and 6px corners. This is the whole of "the input looks dysfunctional". One attribute fixes it.

The close button is at the top left

It is position: static in a flex column, so it takes its own 34px row above the card and sits at the left edge. Its own comment says it "floats over the body". Every system checked puts it top-right, out of the flow. That row is also the empty band above the title.

Seven gaps in one dialog

Measured top to bottom: 32 dead band · 8 mark→title · 4 title→callout · 8 callout→body · 12 body→label · 4 label→input · 20 to the footer. Each part carries its own margin instead of the column carrying one gap.

The dialog grows 80px for the field

A typed word switches the width from 480 to 560, so the same delete is two different sizes depending on whether it asks you to type. Published widths sit at 480–600, so either is fine — but not both.

Verified in the browser at localhost:5173 with getComputedStyle and getBoundingClientRect; sources: Material Web _dialog.scss, Carbon modal style page, Primer Dialog.module.css, Fluent react-dialog constants, Apple HIG Alerts, Atlassian modal.

Decision 1 of 3

How much the dialog says

Every option has the frame fixed: close top-right, 24px padding, 8px title→body, 24px before the footer, 480px held. Only the words differ. No system checked pairs a title with a restating callout and a restating sentence; Apple is explicit that the title carries the meaning and the message exists only if it adds value.

Decision 3 of 3

When to ask for typing at all

Today every policy delete demands the word, including one the dialog itself says "decides no sign-ins" — I hit that case in the build. Nielsen Norman: reserve typing for the rare and dangerous or it becomes another habit. Cloudscape's test is whether the thing can't be recreated or deleting breaks something else. There is no published study showing typed confirmation reduces mistaken deletions — it is convention, so it should be spent where it buys something.
