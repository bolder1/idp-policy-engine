# Policy Engine film v2 — architecture (sixth cut)

The Policy Engine demo film. Same two-pass pipeline as v1 (`capture.mjs` drives the real
app on a stopped clock and writes pictures + a timeline; `render.mjs` composites in a
browser page, synthesises audio, muxes) with these additions, as they stand after the
sixth cut (the cut's own changes are summarised in the next section):

1. **Voice-over** — every line is generated by `edge-tts` before capture, so the recorder
   paces each beat to the spoken line, and the subtitle reveals words as they are said.
2. **Subtitles in a band** — a 100 px band under the product window holds every
   subtitle; the window is scaled to fit above it, so nothing on screen is ever covered.
3. **Bookends of type and a card scene** instead of picture slides — the product's name
   and one sentence on the film's own ground, a cut, then the finished rule card rising
   in (a take in *card mode*: only the card is shown) and read part by part with the
   AND / OR showcase, then a push into the live console. (The fifth cut's device intro
   and live rebuild of the card are gone — see *Sixth cut*.)
4. **Lite edition only.** Full-only functions (Check, Break-in test, What changes, the
   publish gate) are not filmed. The film ends in Lite's *Review & Save*.
5. **Shortcut keycaps** — a chord or named key shows just its caps (top centre of the
   window); typing shows nothing. Cherry MX Blue sound on every key, a real mouse-click
   model, music that ducks under the voice.
6. **Quiet highlights** — a spotlight dims the rest (`rgba(33,37,41,.35)`) inside a 2 px
   slate hairline, and the camera frames it on its own unless the take says `cam: false`.
7. **Light and dark tooling** from the same script — `--theme dark` at capture, the
   compositor's chrome retokenised under `data-theme="dark"`. (The sixth cut is a light
   film; its bookends and insets are light only and dark is neither designed nor checked.)
8. **Bookends of type on the ground** (`slides-v4.js`): transparent slides over the
   compositor's `#bg`; the intro cuts to the card rising in, the outro holds its last frame.
9. **The card's lighting and AND / OR annotation** — one part lit at a time behind a
   veil, then brackets on the card and a truth table beside it arriving in the order the
   narrator says them, driven by `cardParts` / `cardLight` / `cardAnno` / `truthStep`
   events from the take.
10. **Explainer insets** — five small SVG illustrations, one per concept the film explains,
   played beside the product while the window shelves aside (`explain` events).
11. **Filmed against the current build** — `5221ea1` + the run-build worktree's changes
   (see `storyboard/INVENTORY-2026-09-14.md`): a locked vertical board, outcome-first
   Then, *Add people*, a flat attribute list, pending condition groups, and a board
   walkthrough that must be suppressed by seeding `idp.board-tour.seen`.

## Sixth cut (14 Sep 2026) — what changed, by file

The spec is `storyboard/DESIGN-v6.md` (it wins over any report or beat sheet); the script
as a document is `SCRIPT-v6.md`; the beats are `storyboard/BEATS-v6.md`. Owner's brief: the
product's name and one sentence, then the AND / OR showcase; a minimal light outro; small
illustrations wherever a concept (not a control) is explained; the film is about creating a
policy, clear and to the point. Non-negotiables: 1920×1080, 30 fps, light only, no stage,
no device frame, no gradients, no `easeOutBack`, DM Sans only, every value a pure function
of the frame number.

- **Bookends — `compose/slides-v4.js`, `compose/slides-v4.css`** replace `slides-v3.*`
  (deleted; `index.html` links v4 and `explain.*`). `hook` and `outro` are type on the
  compositor's own ground: the lockup, **Policy Engine**, one line (and the address in the

  settling as it lands. The intro's exits are keyed to `params.cues['hk.1']`'s end (never
  before 7.0 s) so a longer voice is never cut off; it ends on ≥ 0.6 s of bare ground and
  **cuts**. The outro starts under the `save` take's dissolve and holds its last frame.

  the same names.
- **Card prelude — rise-in.** A card-mode take's `prelude` (0.6 s) is drawn by the engine
  as frame 0 — the finished card — rising in from bare ground: alpha `easeOut(k)`, a 16 px

## Timeline events (what a take's `events[]` may contain)

| type | fields | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `caption` | `f, id, text, dur` | A spoken line. `id` keys the VO; `dur` = last spoken word + 0.3 s. `text` is the line as written. |
| `click` | `f, x, y, rect, hint, label` | A click; the compositor draws the target outline before and ripples after. |
| `focus` | `f, rect|null, zoom, pad, lead, dur` | A scripted camera move onto a rect (or back to rest). |
| `spotlight` | `f, rect, label, dur, pad, radius, cam?` | Dim everything but the rect inside a 2 px hairline; the camera frames it unless a `focus` is holding **or `cam: false`** (every spotlight under an explainer shelf — the field is only written when false). **Returns at once** — hold before changing the page, or the highlight outlives its subject. |
| `callout` | `f, rect, text, side, dur` | A labelled pointer. |
| `key` | `f, key, label?, dur` | A key or chord (`'a'`, `'Control+z'`, `'Alt+ArrowDown'`, `'?'`). Only chords / named keys / `?` draw keycaps; single characters draw nothing. |
| `sfx` | `f, sfx, gain?, pan?` | `click mxblue whoosh riser chime success thump pop marker boing land sparkle swoosh`. |
| `cardRect` | `f, rect` | The tracked card's rect (app px, 0.1 px) on the frame `f` — emitted by `step()` whenever it changes while `rec.track(selector)` is set. Drives card mode. |
| `cardMode` | `f, on` | Card mode on/off; the compositor blends mask ↔ window over 0.9 s. A card take starts on. |
| `cardFreeze` | `f, on` | Hold the card's picture (a dialog or popover covers it); off → 0.4 s crossfade back to live. **Not emitted by the sixth-cut card take** — the rule is finished before frame 0; the compositor still honours it in old captures. |
| `cardParts` | `f, parts: { title, who[], cond, first, group, block, then[] }` | Rects (app px) of the card's parts, measured once after the cut. A value is a `Rect` or, for a part drawn as more than one row (`who`, `then`), a `Rect[]`. `first` / `group` / `block` place the brackets; the rest are what `cardLight` lights. |
| `cardLight` | `f, part` | Light one part behind the ground-coloured veil, its label pill beside it: `'title' \| 'who' \| 'cond' \| 'group' \| 'then'` (`cond` and `group` both label **If**); `'parts'` = the three labels alone, no veil; `null` clears. Veil out 0.20 s, in 0.35 s. Hidden while the annotation is up or card mode is off. |
| `cardAnno` | `f, on, stage?` | The AND / OR annotation in two stages: `'and'` — the card slides 210 px left, the outer bracket + **AND** label and the truth panel arrive; `'both'` — the inner bracket + **OR** label join. Each stage eases 0.5 s; an event without `stage` means `'both'`; `on: false` eases everything down and the card slides back. |
| `truthStep` | `f, rows` | Light rows 1..n (0–4) of the truth table; a row's lighting time is the first event with `rows ≥ i`; for 0.6 s after, the expression's parenthesis group takes the row's true / false tint. |

| `mascot`, `emote`, `screen`, `click.by` | — | Retired. Not emitted while `MASCOT_ON` is false; ignored by the compositor if present in old captures. |

## Voice-over

`storyboard/lines.mjs`:

```js
export const VOICE = { name: 'en-IN-NeerjaExpressiveNeural', rate: '+10%', pitch: '+0Hz' }
export const LINES = { 'hk.1': 'Policy Engine. Who may sign in, on what conditions, and what they must prove.', /* ... */ }
export const CHAPTERS = [{ id: 'create', n: '01', title: 'A new policy', kicker: 'Workday — Finance adaptive access.' }, /* ... */]
```

`audio/vo.py <lines.json> <outDir> [--voice ... --rate ... --pitch ... --ffmpeg ...]` — for
each id whose text|voice|rate|pitch hash is not already in `<id>.json`:
`edge_tts.Communicate(text, voice, rate, pitch, boundary='WordBoundary')`, stream to collect
audio + word events, save mp3 → ffmpeg → 48 kHz mono 16-bit wav, RMS envelope at 30 Hz,
write `{ id, text, hash, voice, rate, pitch, duration, words:[{t,dur,text}], env:[…] }`.

`lib/vo.mjs`: `await ensureVO({ lines, voice, dir })` (spawns python, throws with the first
stderr line on failure) and `loadVO(dir)` → `Map<id, meta>`. Changing `VOICE` re-speaks
every line (~95 s for the 57 lines of the sixth cut); changing one line's text re-speaks
that line only, since the hash is of text + voice + rate + pitch. The recorder's
`wordTime(id, re)` reads the same word boundaries, so a compositor event can land on the
word that names it (the OR bracket on "Inside", the truth rows on "Both" and "Neither").

The recorder's `say(id)`: emits the caption with `dur = lastWordEnd + 0.3` — the service
pads every clip with ~0.85 s of silence after the last word, so the pace follows the
words, not the file — and remembers `talkUntil`; `await rec.settle(extra = 0.35)` steps
until `talkUntil + extra`. Word boundaries lead the audio by ~65 ms; the EDL shifts them
(`WORD_LEAD`). Word texts arrive without punctuation; the compositor aligns the written
line to them by letters (`alignWords`) so the subtitle shows the script as written.

Slides with `lines` are laid out by the EDL — `lead`, each line's spoken length + `gap`,
`tail` — and receive `params.cues[id] = { at, dur }`, so a slide's motion keys itself to
the voice.

The audio pass places each wav at `t`, peak-normalises the voice bus to −3 dBFS, and ducks
the music by up to 7 dB with a 150 ms attack / 600 ms release follower.

## Compositor

Layers, bottom to top: `#bg` (ground, themed — the only ground on screen; every slide is
transparent) · `#stage` (product window, N stages during a push) · `#ov` (cursor, click
cues, spotlight dim + hairline, tags, callouts, keycaps, the card veil and part labels, the
AND / OR brackets and truth panel, the explainer inset) · `#slides` · `#hud` (progress
rail) · `#cap` (the subtitle band).

**Geometry.** `CAP_BAND = 100`. The window sits at `top 16`, title bar 34, app picture
`934` tall (1440×900 → 1494×934, ×1.038), leaving the band beneath. The camera zooms
inside that picture; whatever it does, the stage is clipped above the band
(`drawStage` clip; the spotlight dim stops there too).

**Caption band.** `.cap`: centred pill (`width: max-content`, max 1560 px — an absolutely
positioned box at `left: 50%` would otherwise shrink-wrap to half the frame), themed with
the tag tokens, DM Sans 500 29 px; unspoken words at the dim token, spoken words full.
One row for lines under ~100 characters; the script keeps to that.

**Camera clamp.** `clampCam` keeps the picture filling the stage exactly: top at the app
area's top, bottom on the caption band (`loY = (O.y − WIN.ay)/s`, `hiY = APP.h − (WIN.ay +
WIN.ah − O.y)/s`). The earlier formula let a zoomed picture stop short of the band, so the
window looked shorter while zoomed — the "height bug".

**Camera.** Explicit `focus` keys first. Then every `spotlight` that is not already framed
(camera at rest, rect smaller than 60 % of the app) gets an auto key: ease to
`fit(rect, 1.28, pad 90)` over 0.9 s, hold `dur + 0.35`, ease back — the return is
skipped when the next spotlight or a scripted move begins within the hold (chaining).
While a scripted frame is held, a spotlight that lies *inside* that picture leaves the
camera alone; one that lies outside it (the If panel grows under a frame taken when it
was short — the group in chapter 04) pans the camera to it at the frame's own zoom, then
returns to the scripted frame. Before this the group spotlight sat under the caption band.

**Highlight.** `#ov-spot` dims (`--spot-dim: rgba(33,37,41,.35)`) everything but the
rounded hole; `.ov-glow` is a 2 px hairline (`--spot-line`) around it and nothing else —
the fifth cut's turning conic gradient and `.ov-ambient` glow are gone (`.ov-ambient` is
`display: none`); the tag label sits above. Every spotlight also emits a `chime`.

**Explainer shelf.** `shelfAt(take, f)` scans the take's `explain` events: while one is
live the product picture and every overlay ease to `xf.s ×0.82, xf.dx +280` (INOUT-B,
0.5 s in from 0.25 s before the inset, 0.4 s out after `dur + 0.6`), composed with any push
on the same `xf`; `#ov-explain` (z 19) is built once per id from `window.EXPLAIN[id]` and
positioned at (105, 409) / (1395, 409) with `translateY((1−kIn)·16 + kOut·8)` and opacity
`kEnv·alpha`; `EXPLAIN[id].update(el, kEnv, t − a)` animates the inside from `t`. The
caption band and `#bg` are untouched. `cameraTrack` ignores spotlights with `cam: false`,
so the camera stays at rest under a shelf (else its 1.28× auto-zoom would make the picture
1905 px wide and overflow the frame).

**Card lighting.** `#ov-card-veil` (SVG, z 17, before `#ov-anno`): one evenodd path in
`rgba(242,244,247,.72)` — the card box rounded `12·m.s` minus one hole per lit rect (padded
6 px, rounded 8 px) — and a second path stroking each hole `#eb5424` at 0.9, 1.5 px. Three
fixed pills `#ov-partlbl-who / -if / -then` (white, 1 px `#e4e8ec`, radius 10, 26/600) sit
with their right edge 22 px left of the first hole, vertically centred on it; enter fade +
10 px rise 0.3 s, exit 0.2 s. `cardTrack` keeps `light[f] = { part, k }` (k 1→0 over
0.20 s for the old part, 0→1 over 0.35 s for the new), `annoAnd` / `annoOr` (0.5 s each, by
`stage`) and `truth[f] = { rows, at[] }`; `drawCardLight` runs only while
`card.blend > 0.5` and `annoAnd < 0.05`.

**Keycaps.** `key` events whose key is a chord, a named key or `?` draw `<kbd>` caps
(`Ctrl`, `Alt`, `↑↓←→`, `Del`, `Esc`, `⌫`, `\`, `?`) joined by `+`, top centre of the
window (`top: 46px`), dropping in over 0.15 s and leaving 0.3 s after the event's `dur`;
the caps sink for the first 0.18 s (`is-down`). The caps are **flat** since the sixth cut:
66 px, radius 14, `--key-bg` on a 1 px `--key-bd` hairline with a solid `--key-side`, no
tray behind them. Single characters (typing) draw nothing.

**Progress rail.** `#hud-rail`: a flat 5 px bar along the frame's foot (`--rail-bg`, fill
`--rail-fill-a`), filled from `edl.contentStart` to the end, one white tick per chapter
(`chapters[].at`); on during takes (`railOn`), hidden while a banner covers the stage.

**Card mode.** A take item with `mode: 'card'` (film-v2: `take('card', null, { mode: 'card',
prelude, out: 'push', music: 'board' })`) is drawn through `cardTrack(take)`: per frame the
exact `cardRect` (a step function of the events), a mask blend eased from `cardMode`
events (0.9 s), and a camera that follows the card with a ~0.22 s lag — `cardCam(rect)` =
centre of the rect, `z = min(1000 / (w·s0), 850 / (h·s0))`, never clamped to the app.
`drawStage` lerps the window box toward `m.rect(card.rect)` by the blend, fades the title
bar with it and clips the picture to the result; cursor and click cues hide while the blend
is over ½ (`uiHidden`). `prelude` seconds before the recording: frame 0 — the finished
card — **rises in** from bare ground (`look.alpha = easeOut(k)`, `xf.dy += (1−k)·16`, no
overlay, no chrome); the mask radius is `lerp(14·m.s / WIN.s0, 12·m.s, blend)`, the card's
own 12 app px in card mode. A take may `out: 'push'` (both stages drawn with `xf.dx` during
the overlap) or `out: 'fade'` (`look.alpha *= 1 − easeInOut(p)` over `tout`, the overlay
dropped past the midpoint, the next slide already underneath). `COMPOSE.cardHold(take)` and
`cardTarget(rect)` are still exported for a slide that wants the card's screen rect; since
the device intro went, none does.

**Slides.** `slideDef(item)` prefers `SLIDES3`, then `SLIDES2`, then `SLIDES`. A slide is
`{ preload?(item, edl), build(el, params, edl), update(el, t, dur, params) }`; `preload`
runs in `init` so pictures are decoded before the first frame. Slides may borrow the
product's pictures: `COMPOSE.frameUrl(take, 'first'|'last'|index)`, `takeEvents(take)`,
`takeFrames(take)`.

**The bookends (`slides-v4.js`, `slides-v4.css`).** Transparent sections over `#bg`;
no pictures, no `preload`. Intro `hook`: the lockup (0.8×, top 280), **Policy Engine**
(128/600, top 472) and the sub-claim (40/400, top 660) fade in rising 12 / 20 / 12 px at
0.6 / 1.2 / 2.4 s (OUT-P fade, OUT-B rise; the name's tracking −0.010 → −0.025 em); they
leave in reverse from `X = max(7.0, E + 0.2)` where `E = cues['hk.1'].at + dur`; ≥ 0.6 s of
bare ground; `out: 'cut'`. Outro: lockup (top 250), name (112, top 442), *Access policy, in
plain English.* (36, top 614), `idp.xecurify.com` (30, top 710) at 0.6 / 1.0 / 2.2 / 3.4 s;
nothing leaves, the last frame is held. A ~20-line cubic-bezier evaluator gives the named
curves; `life(el, t, enter, rise, leave, lift)` drives every element.

**The AND / OR annotation (`anno.js`, `drawAnno`).** `truthHtml(rule)` builds the panel
*How the rule reads* — the expression with tinted `AND` / `OR` keyword pills and the
parenthesised group in `.ov-truth__group`, the sub-line, and a four-row table with
`data-row` — data-driven with the Finance rule as its default. `drawAnno` maps the outer
bracket (`#1189d4`, from `first` to the group's foot) and the AND label from `annoAnd`,
the inner bracket (`#b07a00`) and the OR label from `annoOr`, and the panel's row opacity
and true / false tints from `truth`, through the card camera every frame.

**Push transitions.** `push banner` (between chapters): a full-width band (number, title
84 px, kicker) slides in from the right over 0.45 s, holds, slides out left over 0.45 s;
the stage beneath swaps from the previous take's last frame to the next take's first
frame at the midpoint, blurred. `push out` (card scene → console, or any slide with
`out: 'push'`): the outgoing picture translates to −W while the next enters from +W,
eased over 0.8 s; the EDL adds a whoosh.

**Theme.** `render.mjs --theme dark` sets `<html data-theme="dark">`. `compose.css` and
`slides-v2.css` define their colours twice (`:root` light, `:root[data-theme='dark']`
dark); `slides-v4.css` and `explain.css` are light only and the wordmark is no longer
inverted, so the sixth cut is a light film — the dark path renders but is not designed
for. Canvas drawing (window chrome) reads its colours from CSS variables at init.

## Sound models (`audio/synth.mjs`)

- **mxblue** — press: click-jacket tick (band-passed noise 3–7 kHz, 2.5 ms, centre
  frequency ±12 % per key, seeded) then 12 ms later the bottom-out (low-passed noise
  burst + 1.1–1.4 kHz resonance, 7 ms); release 90–140 ms later, a softer tick at +15 %.
  Space, Enter, Backspace, Shift: deeper bottom-out. −16 dBFS peak per key.
- **click** (mouse) — press: 3 ms transient with 3–5 kHz emphasis + a 280 Hz body knock
  (12 ms), release 70 ms later at 60 %. −18 dBFS. Panned by cursor x (±0.35).
- **marker** — a 1.9–3.1 kHz partial with 6 Hz vibrato and noisy breath, gated by the
  stroke length; −24 dBFS.
- **pop**, **sparkle** (three bell partials glissando up), **swoosh**, **whoosh**,
  **riser**, **thump**, **chime**, **success**; **boing** / **land** (unused now).
- **Music** — 100 BPM, D major → B minor lift. Sections: `board` (the intro slide and the
  card scene: pad + soft pluck, no kick), `demo` (groove, pluck thinned, under every take),
  `banner` (fill + hit), `outro` (open, release ×2.6 so it rings under the held last
  frame); `hook` (kick on every beat, bright pluck, riser) is defined but no longer called
  for by the film. Voice ducking as above. All deterministic.

Sound cues (EDL, sixth cut): the intro's `sfx` thump at 1.2 s (gain .45) as the name lands;
a thump 0.30 s into the card take (gain .35) as the card lands from its rise-in; a whoosh
when `cardMode` goes off (the mask opens) and another as the card pushes into the console;
a `pop` 0.30 s after each `explain` (gain .5); a thump on each `focus`; a `chime` on each
spotlight; `click` / `mxblue` per click and key; swoosh — thump — swoosh on each banner;
`success` on Confirm & Save; the outro's thump at 1.0 s (gain .4). A slide that starts under
a dissolving take (the outro) gets no transition sound; the last item is not faded.

## Capture

`capture.mjs`: `--theme light|dark`, `--edition lite` default, `--work .work/<theme>`,
`--vo .work/vo`. Before booting the app: `ensureVO({ lines: LINES, voice: VOICE, dir })`.
Dark: click *Switch to dark theme* and assert `data-theme="dark"`. The persona pill `.bpb`
stays hidden. Every take asserts its end state (card count, slot numbers, dialog gone) —
a landed click proves nothing. A `spotlight()` returns immediately: `hold()` for its
duration before anything navigates away. Capture order is `create → start → who → if →
then → canvas → save` in one session, then `card` last (shown second); the sixth cut's
re-capture list is `--only create,who,if,then,canvas,save,card` (`start` is unchanged and
runs dry). `--dry` also re-synthesises every line whose text changed.
